Om Namah Shivaya

A sacred home for peace, devotion and divine energy — where daily rites, abishegam and Rudra chanting keep an unbroken thread of tradition alive in the heart of Brickfields.

Sri Shivan Alayam Brickfields stands as a symbol of peace, divine energy and spiritual devotion. Daily poojas, Rudra chanting, special abishegam and sacred festivals are conducted with devotion and tradition, welcoming every devotee who crosses the threshold.
